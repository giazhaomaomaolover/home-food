# HomeState

Our private cooking app: 81 recipes, a shared weekly plan, and a shopping list that adds itself up.
Open it on your phone and use "Add to Home Screen". Sign in with your email (a 6-digit code, no password).

The recipe text and pictures are public in this repository; the plan, shopping list, favorites and health data live in Supabase behind sign-in.
Built from the source in `home-food-app-source.zip` with `python3 build_web.py`.
